EM Playgroundby Bishal Ramdam

See radio waves and electricity move.

Two interactive labs built on real physics. The fields are computed from Maxwell's equations as you play, so what you see spreading, bouncing and flowing is what the fields actually do. Nothing to install; it runs in your browser, even on a phone.

Lab 1

Radio Wave Bench

A live Maxwell wave tank: place antennas, draw metal and glass, watch waves diffract, interfere and get guided.

  • FM, Wi-Fi and 5G beam steering
  • Waveguides, ionosphere bounce, wavelets
  • Wi-Fi signal map of a house
Open the bench → Lab 2

Wire Energy Playground

Energy flows around the wire, not inside it. Follow the Poynting vector from a torch bulb to a 765 kV line.

  • Twisted pair, coax, house wiring, 3-phase cables
  • 50 to 1,000 km lines: Ferranti, SIL, collapse
  • The corona challenge, with sound
